The dataset was generated by the Material Studio project using Blender EEVEE,
PBR texture maps from Poly Haven and ambientCG, and a controlled sphere scene
with camera, lighting, and object-orientation variation. It contains synthetic
renders, not collected photographs or human-subject data.

Source-map provenance was checked against official provider records. Evidence
covers 316 development maps (315 used and one unused) and 63 test maps, under
the providers' CC0 terms:

- Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/license
- ambientCG: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

The archive includes source attribution, map hashes, verification records,
render manifests, configuration, and source-family assignments under
`provenance/`. The test renders use Blender 5.1.1; the historical development
renderer version was not recorded. Generation uses seed 62642 and retains
per-render parameters. PNG ancillary metadata was removed without changing
decoded pixels.

The current image index is `metadata/manifest.csv`: 3,150 development images
and 630 evaluated study test images. Keep `source_group` intact during
development splits. Historical `original_split` values record lineage only.
Known groups do not prove complete physical independence.

Publisher: Dian Brown. Rendered images and project-authored dataset metadata:

and provenance-code notices remain separately identified in the package.
